LifeOS Privacy Notice

Beta template — legal review required before public launch.

LifeOS stores account, task, calendar, email-derived, memory, notification, billing, and diagnostic information needed to provide the service. Connected account access is limited to the permissions granted by the user. External actions require user approval where applicable.

Users can export or delete their LifeOS account data from Settings. Production deployments must fill in operator identity, retention periods, subprocessor list, jurisdiction-specific rights, and contact information before launch.
