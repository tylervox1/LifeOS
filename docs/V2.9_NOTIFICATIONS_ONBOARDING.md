# LifeOS V2.9

## Browser push
The app stores Web Push subscriptions per user. The worker can deliver Daily Brief and proactive-alert notifications through VAPID-configured Web Push.

## Onboarding
First-run wizard:
1. product introduction
2. Google connection
3. notification preferences
4. completion

## Recovery
Settings shows Gmail/Calendar sync state. Failed providers can reset their cursor and enqueue a fresh sync.

## Mobile groundwork
`capacitor.config.json` plus Capacitor dependencies/scripts are included. Native iOS/Android projects are not generated automatically; those require the platform SDKs on the developer machine.
